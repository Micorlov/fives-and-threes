Privacy Policy for Fives and Threes

Last updated: August 27, 2026

Fives and Threes ("we", "us") is a free offline domino game. This policy explains what data the app handles. In short: the app collects nothing and sends nothing anywhere.

Information We Collect

None. We do not collect, transmit, or receive any personal or device information. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, and no server component.

Data Stored on Your Device

  • Game progress: the current match and hand, so you can close the app and resume.
  • Statistics: matches won, average score per hand, best single placement and scoring rate.
  • Settings: difficulty, sound, haptics, and reduced-motion preferences.

This data is written only to your device's local app storage. It never leaves your device, and we have no way to read it. Uninstalling the app removes it permanently.

What We Don't Do

  • We do not collect personal information of any kind.
  • We do not use analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs.
  • We do not serve advertising.
  • We do not collect location data.
  • We do not process payments; the app has no in-app purchases.
  • We do not share or sell data, because we do not have any.

Data Deletion

Because all data stays on your device, you can delete it entirely by uninstalling the app or clearing the app's storage in Android settings. No request to us is needed.

No Real Money Gambling

This game is intended for entertainment purposes only. There is no betting, no wagering, and no ability to win real money or prizes.

Children's Privacy

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children. It is a traditional pub domino game and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new "last updated" date.
